## Short description

HRIS is an open research framework and practical toolkit for studying interaction-shaped behavior, conversational shorthand, trajectory reconstruction, and long-horizon human–AI collaboration.

## Short social post

What changes when a human and an AI develop reusable shorthand over repeated interaction? HRIS is an open research framework for studying interaction-shaped behavior and trajectory reconstruction. The free Starter Kit includes a 20–30 minute comparison experiment, worksheet, and results template. Positive, negative, and null results are welcome.

https://github.com/HudsonCapsuleAI/hris-framework/releases/latest

## Academic or professional post

I have released the HRIS Research Starter Kit, an open exploratory protocol for comparing a history-conditioned human–AI interaction with a fresh-session control. The goal is to make claims about interaction-shaped behavior more testable, document alternative explanations, and invite replication—including negative and null results. The repository also includes the broader HRIS framework, 40-paper index, diagrams, citation metadata, and CC BY 4.0 licensing.

https://github.com/HudsonCapsuleAI/hris-framework

## Sustainable visibility checklist

- Link the release from the HRIS website and relevant researcher profiles.
- Share one visual framework with one concrete research question.
- Invite critique and null results, not only agreement.
- Respond constructively to issues and replications.
- Publish meaningful releases when protocols, datasets, or papers change.
- Record external presentations, citations, and replications in the repository.
- Avoid unsolicited bulk messages or irrelevant cross-posting.
