# Contributing to HRIS

HRIS welcomes constructive critique, replications, protocol improvements, conceptual analysis, visual explanations, positive findings, negative findings, and null results.

## Ways to participate

- Run or adapt the [HRIS Research Starter Kit](starter-kit/).
- Open an issue describing a result, limitation, alternative explanation, or proposed experiment.
- Improve documentation through a pull request.
- Cite the framework in related research and link back to this repository.
- Share the repository with human–AI interaction, HCI, LLM, and open-science communities.

## Reporting an experiment

Please include enough context for another person to understand or reproduce the observation:

- AI system, model/version, interface, and date
- Original task and transfer task
- Candidate shorthand cue
- History-conditioned and fresh-control procedures
- Number of trials
- Scores or qualitative observations
- Failures, drift, null results, and alternative explanations
- Any changes made to the starter protocol

Do not treat an exploratory observation as proof of persistent identity, hidden memory, consciousness, or a general causal mechanism.

## Privacy and research ethics

Do not publish private conversations, personal identifiers, confidential information, protected health information, or participant data without appropriate permission and safeguards. Redact unnecessary content. If human participants are involved in formal research, follow the requirements of your institution and jurisdiction.
